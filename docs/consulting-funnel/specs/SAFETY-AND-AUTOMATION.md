# Channel permissions, approval & safety

**Default:** public-source discovery and draft-only. No application, bid, client contact, paid platform credits, contract acceptance or account automation without specific user authorization and a permitted method.

## Three levels

1. **Auto-discover and draft:** search publicly accessible, allowed sources; normalize; verify; score; prepare evidence-backed proposal. No login automation required.
2. **Prepare and open:** one-tap deep link and clipboard-ready draft; owner reviews and submits manually in platform UI, records outcome.
3. **Selective delegated outreach:** optional, separately approved only for channels where terms/API permit it; allowlist, rate limits, spend caps, kill switch, logs and precise approval scope. No presumption this is supported by Upwork, Contra or any other source.

## Controls for future slices

| Control | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Channel policy registry | record reviewed policy URL, date, reviewer, permitted reads/writes. |
| Human approval | authorize exact destination, proposal body and financial commitment before any send. |
| Scope & honesty | no fake past experience, made-up buyer needs, guessed price guarantees or unreviewed delivery commitments. |
| Rate/spend limits | per-platform daily count, max application fees/credits, duplicates blocked. |
| Incident stop | disable source/automated action immediately; record failure and last known state. |
| Audit | who approved, exact payload hash, destination, timestamp, outcome; protect personal data. |
| Data minimization | no credentials/tokens/private customer messages in GitHub or screenshots. |
| Platform changes | block writes until compliance re-reviewed. |

Do not attempt to evade bot detection, impersonate users, scrape protected marketplace content, or send unsolicited bulk spam.
